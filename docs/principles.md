# The Digital Observatory Principles

1. The observatory is alive.

2. Nothing should feel like webpage navigation.

3. Every interaction should have purpose.

4. Curiosity is rewarded.

5. Technology should disappear behind the experience.

6. Visitors are observers, not users.

7. Polaris guides but never controls.

8. Every light, sound and animation must communicate something.

9. Silence is part of the experience.

10. Build experiences, not interfaces.
